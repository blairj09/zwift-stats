Zwift Stats for Mac: XP and Drops per ride, week and route, with your Zwift Companion data and optional Strava notes.

**Install:** download `Zwift-Stats-macOS.zip`, unzip it, and drag **Zwift Stats** into Applications. The app isn't notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS will say it can't check it for malware. Click **Done**, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to Zwift Stats. After that it opens normally. Allow it to access your Documents folder when asked, so it can read `Documents/Zwift`.

Runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 11 or later.
